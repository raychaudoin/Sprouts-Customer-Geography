# MODEL-08: Wisconsin Evidence Expansion Strategy

## Purpose and accepted disposition

MODEL-08 is an accepted, disclosure-safe controlling strategy for future Wisconsin evidence generations. It is a strategy record, not an execution authorization.

## Wisconsin-first evidence strategy

Normal protected intake is one complete Wisconsin Sprouts annual forecast-generation vintage containing all Wisconsin seed points supplied in that release, with market/MSA identity retained. Arbitrary or cherry-picked individual seed submissions are not normal intake. Prior vintages remain immutable, and later annual releases are separate evidence generations.

## Cross-vintage identity and evidence roles

Physical-location identity resolution is target-blind across vintages. A new Seed Point ID does not itself establish a new physical location; repeated underlying locations are temporal evidence. Genuinely new Milwaukee locations are prospective Milwaukee evidence, while Madison remains external Wisconsin-market evidence. Ambiguous identity is quarantined.

Target-consumed evidence is development/reference evidence. Existing genuinely new Milwaukee and Madison targets remain sealed. Other-state evidence is deferred. A future annual vintage requires successor preregistration before targets are opened.

## Relationship to MODEL-07 and protected-data boundary

MODEL-07 remains closed and inconclusive. This record contains no seed identifiers, location identities, coordinates, addresses, protected workbook details, forecasts, targets, rankings, registries, packages, nonces, hashes, or reconstructable protected lineage.

MODEL-08 does not itself authorize evidence ingestion, target opening, validation execution, or analytical execution.
